# Payments & Billing

_Last updated: 2026-06-01_

## Accepted payment methods

We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and Nimbus Gear gift cards. On orders from $50 to $1,000, you can also pay in 4 interest-free installments with Klarna. We don't accept cash, checks, or cryptocurrency.

## When you're charged

When you place an order, we put a **pre-authorization hold** on your card. We charge the card when the order ships. If an order ships in more than one package, each package is charged separately. Holds on cancelled orders drop off within 3–5 business days, depending on your bank.

## Seeing two charges?

This is usually a pre-authorization hold showing next to the final charge. The hold disappears within 3–5 business days. If both charges still show as posted (not pending) after 5 business days, contact support with your order number.

## Price adjustments

If Nimbus Gear lowers the price of an item within **14 days** of your purchase, we'll refund the difference, once per item. Contact support with your order number to ask for it.

We **don't** match competitors' prices. Price adjustments don't apply to Final Sale, clearance, or flash-sale items.

## Promo codes

You can use one promo code per order. Promo codes can't be used on gift cards or combined with store-credit bonuses, and they can't be added after an order is placed. If a code isn't working, check its expiration date and minimum order amount.

## Gift cards

Gift cards never expire and have no fees. They can't be exchanged for cash (except where the law requires it) and can't be returned. We can replace a lost gift card only if you have the original purchase receipt.

## Sales tax and invoices

We charge sales tax based on the shipping address, as state law requires. You can download a PDF invoice from **Account → Orders → View Invoice**. Guest customers can ask support to email them an invoice.
