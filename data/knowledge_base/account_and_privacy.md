# Account & Privacy

_Last updated: 2026-04-20_

## Do I need an account?

No. With guest checkout, you can track guest orders on the **Order Lookup** page using the order number and the email you used at checkout. If you create an account later with the same email, your past guest orders show up in it.

## Password reset

Click **Forgot password** on the sign-in page. The reset link works for **30 minutes**. If the email doesn't arrive, check your spam folder. Support can't reset your password for you and can't see it.

## Changing your email or details

You can update your name, email, phone number, and saved addresses in **Account → Settings**. When you change your email, we send a verification link to the new address, and the change takes effect once you confirm it.

## Verifying your identity with support

To protect your account, support staff and the virtual assistant will ask for your **order number and the email address on the order** before discussing any order details. Nimbus Gear will **never** ask for your password, your full card number, or your card's security code.

## Marketing emails

You can unsubscribe using the link at the bottom of any marketing email, or in **Account → Settings → Notifications**. Order and shipping emails can't be turned off, because they're about your purchases.

## Deleting your account and data

To request deletion, email **privacy@nimbusgear.example** from the address on your account. We confirm the request within 3 business days and finish deleting your data within **30 days**. The law requires us to keep order and payment records for **7 years** for tax and accounting. Everything else is deleted, including saved addresses, wishlists, chat history, and marketing preferences.

## Chat transcripts

We keep conversations with the virtual assistant for **90 days** to improve service and handle escalations. After that, they're deleted.
