# Warranty & Repairs

_Last updated: 2026-05-10_

## Nimbus-brand products

All Nimbus-brand products (Nimbus Pods, AuraCharge, PowerCell, Nimbus Hub, and TrackTag) come with a **2-year limited warranty** that starts on the delivery date.

**Exception:** Nimbus-brand cables, such as the Braided USB-C Cable, have a **lifetime warranty** for as long as you own them.

## Third-party brands

Products from other brands we sell, such as Kestrel Audio and Halden, are covered by the **manufacturer's warranty**, which is usually 1 year. During the 30-day return window, we handle defects for you directly. After that, contact the manufacturer. We can provide proof of purchase from your order history.

## What's covered

The warranty covers manufacturing defects in materials and workmanship under normal use. For example: a charger that stops charging, earbuds that won't pair even after a reset, or buttons that stop responding.

## What's not covered

- Accidental damage (drops, cracks, crushing), unless you have Nimbus Care+
- Water or liquid damage beyond the product's IP rating (Nimbus Pods Pro are rated IPX4, meaning splash-resistant, not waterproof)
- Normal battery wear. A battery that keeps at least **80% of its capacity after 500 charge cycles** is working as designed.
- Loss or theft
- Products that were modified, opened, or repaired by anyone other than Nimbus Gear
- Cosmetic wear, such as scratches or fading

## How to make a warranty claim

Contact support with:

1. Your order number (or proof of purchase, if you bought the product somewhere else)
2. The serial number, printed on the product or its box
3. A short description of the problem and a photo or video of it

We usually reply within 1 business day. Once your claim is approved, we ship a replacement within **5 business days**. For most items you don't need to send the defective one back. If we do need it, we'll include a prepaid label. If the product has been discontinued, we'll replace it with an equal or better model.

## Nimbus Care+

Nimbus Care+ is optional extended protection for Nimbus-brand products. It costs **$24.99 per product** and must be bought within 30 days of delivery. It extends coverage to **3 years** from delivery and adds **one accidental damage replacement per year**. You can get a refund for Nimbus Care+ within 30 days of buying it, as long as you haven't made a claim.
