# Returns & Refunds

_Last updated: 2026-08-01_

## Return window

You can return most items within **30 days of the delivery date** shown in your order history. The window starts on the delivery date, not the order date. Items delivered more than 30 days ago can't be returned, but Nimbus-brand products may still be covered by the warranty (see Warranty & Repairs).

## What can be returned

- **Unopened items** in their original packaging can always be returned within the window.
- **Opened items** can be returned if they're undamaged and complete: all cables, manuals, accessories, and packaging.
- **In-ear products** (earbuds such as Nimbus Pods Pro and Nimbus Pods Lite) can be returned only if the **hygiene seal** on the ear-tip tray is intact, unless the product is defective.
- **Not returnable:** gift cards, items marked **Final Sale** (including all clearance items), and products with removed or damaged serial numbers.

## Restocking fee

There's no restocking fee for defective items, items damaged in shipping, or items we sent by mistake.

Opened, non-defective items priced **over $150** have a **10% restocking fee**. Items priced $150 or less have no restocking fee.

## Return shipping

- **Defective, damaged, or wrong item:** we email you a prepaid return label at no cost.
- **All other returns:** we still email a prepaid label, and **$6.95** is deducted from your refund.
- Returns must be postmarked within **14 days** after the return is approved, or the return authorization expires.

## How to start a return

1. Sign in, go to **Account → Orders**, choose the order, and select **Request Return**. Guest customers can do this from the **Order Lookup** page with their order number and email.
2. Choose the items and a reason. You'll get a return authorization number (format `RMA-12345`) and a shipping label by email.
3. Pack the items, write the RMA number on the packing slip, and drop the package off with the carrier shown on the label.

## Refund timing

Refunds are processed within **3 business days** after the warehouse receives your return. The money goes back to your original payment method and usually shows up within **5–10 business days**, depending on your bank. PayPal refunds usually show up within 2 business days.

You can choose **store credit** instead of a refund and get a **10% bonus**. For example, a $100 return becomes $110 in store credit. Store credit never expires.

## Exchanges

We don't offer direct exchanges. For a different color or model, return the original item and place a new order. If the item is defective, request a replacement under Warranty & Repairs instead, which is faster.

## Cancelling an order

You can cancel an order while its status is **Processing**. Once it has shipped, it can't be cancelled. Return it after delivery instead. Cancelled orders are never charged, and the pre-authorization hold drops off within 3–5 business days.
