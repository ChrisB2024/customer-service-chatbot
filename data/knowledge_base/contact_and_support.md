# Contact & Support

_Last updated: 2026-03-01_

## Support hours

People are available for support **Monday–Friday, 9:00 AM–6:00 PM Eastern Time**, except on US federal holidays. The virtual assistant is available 24/7.

## How to reach us

- **Email:** support@nimbusgear.example. We reply within 1 business day.
- **Phone:** 1-800-555-0142 (during support hours)
- **Chat:** ask the virtual assistant to connect you with a person. During support hours, someone usually joins within 10 minutes. Outside support hours, the assistant creates a ticket and we email you on the next business day.

## What the virtual assistant can help with

- Questions about our returns, shipping, warranty, payment, and account policies
- Questions about Nimbus-brand products
- Order status and tracking, after you verify your order number and email

## When you're connected to a person

The assistant creates a support ticket and hands you over to a person when:

- You ask for a person
- An item arrived damaged, or you received the wrong item
- A package is lost, or it's marked delivered but still hasn't arrived after 48 hours
- You're making a warranty claim or need an exception to a refund policy
- You suspect fraud or unauthorized charges
- The assistant can't answer your question confidently

The assistant can't issue refunds, change orders, or make exceptions to policies. Only a person can do those things.

## About Nimbus Gear

Nimbus Gear is an online-only store. We don't have physical stores, and in-person pickup isn't available.
