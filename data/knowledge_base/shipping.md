# Shipping & Delivery

_Last updated: 2026-07-15_

## Where we ship

We ship to all 50 US states, Washington D.C., and Canada. We don't ship to other countries, APO/FPO addresses, or freight forwarders.

## Processing time

Orders placed before **2:00 PM Eastern Time, Monday–Friday** ship the same business day. Orders placed after the cutoff, on weekends, or on US federal holidays ship the next business day.

## US shipping options

| Method    | Delivery time (after shipping) | Cost                                   |
|-----------|--------------------------------|----------------------------------------|
| Standard  | 5–7 business days              | $5.99, **free on orders of $50 or more** |
| Expedited | 2–3 business days              | $12.99                                 |
| Overnight | 1 business day                 | $24.99                                 |

Overnight orders must be placed before **1:00 PM Eastern Time** on a business day to ship that day.

PO boxes are supported with Standard shipping only (sent via USPS).

## Canada shipping

Standard shipping to Canada costs **$14.99** and takes **7–12 business days**. There's no free-shipping threshold for Canada, and Expedited and Overnight aren't available. Prices are in US dollars. The carrier may collect import duties and taxes on delivery, and these are the customer's responsibility.

## Carriers and tracking

We ship with UPS and USPS. When your order ships, we email you the carrier and tracking number. The tracking number also appears in **Account → Orders** (or on the Order Lookup page for guests). The first tracking scan can take up to 24 hours to appear.

## Changing your shipping address

You can change the shipping address while the order status is **Processing**. Once the order has shipped, we can't redirect it, but you can ask the carrier for a delivery change on their website.

## Late, lost, or missing packages

- **Marked delivered but not received:** check with neighbors and around your property, then wait **48 hours**, because carriers sometimes mark packages delivered early. If it still hasn't arrived, contact support and we'll open a carrier claim.
- **No tracking updates for 7 days:** contact support and we'll open a trace with the carrier.
- If the carrier confirms the package is lost, we'll ship a replacement at no cost or give you a full refund, whichever you prefer.

## Damaged in transit

If your package arrives damaged, take photos of the box and the item within **48 hours of delivery** and contact support. We'll send a replacement and a prepaid label to return the damaged item.
