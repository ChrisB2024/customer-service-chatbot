# Product FAQ

_Last updated: 2026-08-20_

## Nimbus Pods Pro (wireless earbuds)

- **Battery:** up to 8 hours per charge with noise cancellation (ANC) off, or 6 hours with ANC on. The case adds 32 more hours. Charging for 10 minutes gives about 1.5 hours of playback.
- **Pairing:** with the earbuds in the case and the lid open, press and hold the case button for **3 seconds** until the light flashes white. Then choose "Nimbus Pods Pro" in your phone's Bluetooth settings.
- **Reset:** hold the case button for **15 seconds** until the light flashes amber, then pair again. This fixes most connection problems.
- **Water resistance:** IPX4. Sweat and rain are fine, but don't submerge them or wear them in the shower.
- **Multipoint:** they can connect to two devices at the same time.

## Nimbus Pods Lite

- Up to 6 hours per charge, or 24 hours in total with the case. No active noise cancellation.
- Pairing and reset work the same way as on Nimbus Pods Pro.
- Water resistance: IPX4.

## AuraCharge 65W GaN Charger

- Ports: 2× USB-C and 1× USB-A.
- One USB-C port on its own delivers up to **65W**, which is enough for most 13–14" laptops. With two devices plugged in, the power splits 45W + 20W. With all three, it splits 45W + 10W + 10W.
- It supports USB Power Delivery 3.0 and PPS, so it fast-charges Samsung phones.
- It's normal for the charger to get warm while charging. If it gets too hot to touch or keeps shutting off, stop using it and contact support.

## PowerCell 20K Power Bank

- 20,000 mAh (74 Wh). It charges most phones 4–5 times.
- **Air travel:** allowed in **carry-on bags**, because it's under the airlines' 100 Wh limit. Power banks aren't allowed in checked luggage.
- It charges laptops at up to 30W over USB-C. Recharging it fully takes about 4 hours with a charger of 30W or more.

## Nimbus Hub 7-in-1 USB-C Hub

- Ports: HDMI, 2× USB-A 3.2, USB-C data, USB-C Power Delivery pass-through (up to **100W**), and SD and microSD card readers.
- HDMI supports **4K at 60Hz** on computers with DisplayPort Alt Mode over USB-C. Some older laptops are limited to 4K at 30Hz.
- Base M1 and M2 MacBooks natively support only one external display. That's an Apple limitation, not a defect in the hub.

## TrackTag Bluetooth Tracker

- Works with the free **Nimbus app** (iOS 16+ and Android 10+).
- Bluetooth range is about 60 m (200 ft) in open space. When the tag is out of range, the app shows where it was last seen.
- It uses a replaceable **CR2032** coin battery that lasts about 1 year. The app warns you when the battery is low.
- Water resistance: IP67.

## Braided USB-C Cable (2 m, 240W)

- Supports charging at up to 240W (USB PD 3.1) and data at USB 2.0 speeds (480 Mbps). It **doesn't carry video**. For displays, use a Thunderbolt or USB-C video cable.
- Covered by a lifetime warranty.
